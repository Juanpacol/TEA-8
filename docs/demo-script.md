# Demo script, 30 seconds

For the challenge video (30 seconds at most, English, no NASA logo or branding). Published version: https://youtu.be/9Tsnuw9hfzE (29.9 s, recorded on 2026-11-15). Screens are real recordings of the tool; the voice-over is generated with ElevenLabs.

## Scenes

| Time | Screen | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|
| 0-5.7 s | Hook: the 2012 drought in McLean County, Illinois. | "A dry summer took 44% of the corn. Next summer, will your crops make it?" |
| 5.7-9.9 s | McLean County: three rotations side by side, with what is not known and no best option. | "Compare rotations side by side. You decide." |

## Before recording

1. **Ask answer varies.** The model does not answer the same way each time. On 2026-11-14 the 2012 question gave three verified claims and one marked "Not verified" (the model wrote 23.0 °C; the data says 25.6 °C). Ask again until the shown claims are verified, or keep the "Not verified" label on screen and say that the check caught it. Do not edit a take to hide it.
2. Use the example button, not typed text, so the question is the same.
3. Do not show costs, accounts, keys or the browser's other tabs.
4. Record at desktop width; the phone layout was checked only in a 360 px frame, not on a phone.

## Submission checklist for the video

- 30 seconds at most. If using slides instead: 7 slides at most.
- English voice-over or captions.
- No NASA logo, no NASA branding.
- Any image made with AI carries a visible watermark and is named in the acknowledgements.
- Say in the text that AI tools were used (list each one) and that the answers in the tool are generated by a language model and verified by code.

## Not in this script, on purpose

- No claim of "most scalable" or "best fit" (see `docs/alternatives.md`).
- No yield or profit claim for a rotation: county yields are for corn and soybeans, not per rotation.
